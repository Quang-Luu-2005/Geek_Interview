\section{Scope Strategy}
The assessment explicitly permits a reasonable, documented feature boundary.
The implementation therefore prioritizes customer
booking correctness, a defensible minimal operation workflow, deterministic
local delivery, and evidence under races. Production breadth is not claimed
where the code does not provide it.

\section{Implemented Scope}
The submitted backend implements published-concert browse/detail/category
reads; informational availability; transactional booking creation; optional
voucher application; idempotent replay; owned booking history/detail;
confirm/cancel commands; automatic expiry; operator booking list/detail/manual
transition; draft concert/category creation and publication; scoped voucher
creation; stable errors; health/metrics/OpenAPI endpoints; and audit history.

Full update/delete administration, real payment, notification delivery,
seat-level selection, refunds, and distributed deployment are deliberately
excluded. Chapter~\ref{ch:payment-scope} records the payment boundary in detail.

\section{Critical Business Invariants}
\begin{invariantbox}[INV-01 --- Inventory safety]
For every category, \code{0 <= available\_quantity <= total\_quantity} always
holds, including under concurrent booking attempts.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-02 --- Exactly-once logical reservation]
One user-scoped logical booking intent consumes inventory at most once, even if
the client retries concurrently or after losing a response.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-03 --- Voucher quota safety]
Global voucher usage never exceeds its configured quota and the active
per-user redemption constraint is never violated.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-04 --- Atomic booking creation]
Idempotency claim, inventory reservation, voucher reservation, server pricing,
booking/items, audit history, and replayable result either all commit or all
roll back as implemented by the critical transaction.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-05 --- Exactly-once resource release]
Expired or cancelled reservations restore ticket and eligible voucher capacity
exactly once; repeat worker/customer/operator commands cannot double-release.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-06 --- Historical price integrity]
Changing current ticket or promotion data cannot alter an existing booking's
persisted unit prices or monetary totals.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-07 --- Lifecycle validity]
Every status change follows \code{RESERVED -> CONFIRMED | EXPIRED | CANCELLED};
terminal states cannot be overwritten by a racing command.
\end{invariantbox}

\begin{invariantbox}[INV-08 --- Operation auditability]
Every successful creation or manual/worker lifecycle transition appends status
history with source, reason where applicable, actor when available, and time.
\end{invariantbox}

\section{Non-Goals for the Assessment}
\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
  \item real payment authorization, capture, settlement, reconciliation, refunds, or chargebacks;
  \item seat-map / exact-seat selection;
  \item voucher or concert update/delete workflows beyond implemented creation/publication;
  \item email/SMS delivery and real-time WebSocket availability;
  \item multi-region active-active deployment or full microservice decomposition;
  \item mandatory Redis, Kafka, or Kubernetes as correctness prerequisites.
\end{itemize}
