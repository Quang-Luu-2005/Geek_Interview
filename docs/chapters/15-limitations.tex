\section{Known Limitations and Evolution}

The current identity header, simulated payment, category-level inventory,
process-local rate limiter/metrics, and polling expiry worker are deliberate
assessment trade-offs. Their operational impact and production evolution path
are recorded in \code{docs/ASSUMPTIONS\_SCOPE\_LIMITATIONS.md}. None of these
limitations relaxes the critical invariants: inventory cannot oversell,
idempotent retries cannot double-book, voucher quota cannot over-redeem, and
expiry cannot double-release resources.
