\section{Operation API Scope}
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Representative operation endpoints}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.16\textwidth}p{0.38\textwidth}Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Method} & \textbf{Endpoint} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
GET & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/bookings} & Monitor/filter bookings \\
GET & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/bookings/\{id\}} & Investigate booking and audit detail \\
PATCH & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/bookings/\{id\}/status} & Guarded, reasoned manual transition \\
POST & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/concerts} & Create draft concert \\
POST & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/concerts/\{id\}/ticket-categories} & Create category and initial inventory \\
POST & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/concerts/\{id\}/publish} & Publish a valid future concert \\
POST & \apiendpoint{/api/admin/vouchers} & Create a validated scoped/global voucher \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Operation routes require an elevated operator/admin identity. Booking list
filters are status, concert, user, and creation-time ranges with stable
pagination. Booking detail exposes status history, voucher redemption state,
and idempotency outcome timestamps without request hashes or stored response
bodies.

Manual status commands accept only \code{CONFIRMED}, \code{EXPIRED}, or
\code{CANCELLED} as targets from \code{RESERVED}. The command-specific expiry
guard is evaluated in PostgreSQL; cancellation/expiry release inventory and
voucher quota in the same transaction, and every successful command appends an
\code{OPERATION\_API} audit row with the required reason.

The intentionally minimal catalog scope creates draft concerts, creates ticket
categories with initial inventory, publishes only future concerts with
inventory, and creates validated vouchers. Update/delete APIs are omitted so
published prices, inventory history, and already-redeemed voucher policy cannot
be changed without a separately specified business rule.

\section{Availability Semantics}
Availability returned by read APIs is informational and may become stale immediately under concurrent demand. The authoritative inventory decision always occurs inside \apiendpoint{POST /api/bookings}.
