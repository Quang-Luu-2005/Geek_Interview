\section{Business Context}
Customers browse published concerts, reserve finite category inventory, apply
eligible vouchers, and track a deliberately small booking lifecycle. Operators
and administrators investigate bookings and perform guarded maintenance. The
assessment workload is approximately 300--500 booking requests per minute; the
hard problem is synchronized demand for the same last tickets or voucher units.

\section{Functional Requirements}
``Explicit'' means stated by the brief; ``Derived'' supports the stated user
workflow; ``Engineering'' preserves correctness and operability.

\textbf{Functional requirement list}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{FR-01--FR-04:} browse published concerts, view details, inspect categories/prices, and see availability.
\item \textbf{FR-05--FR-08:} create a timed reservation, apply eligible vouchers, and view own booking detail/history.
\item \textbf{FR-09--FR-11:} let operators/admins monitor, inspect, and perform guarded booking transitions with an audit reason.
\item \textbf{FR-12--FR-14:} create draft concerts/categories, publish valid inventory, and create scoped vouchers.
\item \textbf{FR-15:} define the documented future update/delete workflow for concert or voucher definitions.
\item \textbf{FR-16--FR-19:} confirm/cancel reservations, expire overdue reservations, and release inventory and voucher quota exactly once.
\item \textbf{FR-20:} replay a repeated booking intent idempotently without creating a duplicate booking.
\end{itemize}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}
\textbf{Non-functional requirement list}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{NFR-01--NFR-04:} preserve non-negative inventory, one reservation per logical request, bounded voucher quotas, and atomic booking creation.
\item \textbf{NFR-05--NFR-06:} control flash-sale failures and demonstrate the target 300--500 booking requests per minute locally.
\item \textbf{NFR-07:} ensure lifecycle races produce one valid terminal state with no double release.
\item \textbf{NFR-08:} enforce ownership, role authorization, request validation, and safe configuration handling.
\item \textbf{NFR-09--NFR-10:} keep module boundaries testable and make local setup, reproduction, and observability practical.
\end{itemize}

\section{Engineering Risks and Design Goal}
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\caption{Primary risk-to-design mapping}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{p{0.20\textwidth}p{0.29\textwidth}Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Risk} & \textbf{Failure mode} & \textbf{Engineering response} \\
\midrule
Overselling & Inventory sold beyond capacity & Atomic conditional PostgreSQL update \\
Duplicate retry & Multiple bookings from one intent & User-scoped idempotency key, fingerprint, and replay \\
Voucher abuse & Global/per-user quota exceeded & Atomic quota reservation and redemption constraint \\
Lifecycle race & Conflicting terminal state or double release & Conditional transition from \code{RESERVED} \\
Flash-sale instability & Hot rows and retry storms & Short transactions, controlled errors, limiter, and evidence \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

The design deliberately prioritizes correctness under concurrent and retried
booking requests over architectural breadth.
